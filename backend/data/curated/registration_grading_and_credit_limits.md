## Summary
Explains grading options, standard credit limits, and how students request approval to exceed enrollment limits.

## Common student scenarios
- Wanting to take a course pass/fail  
- Unsure how many credits allowed per semester  
- Attempting to overload courses  

---

## Grading options

### Letter grade (default)
- Counts toward GPA and major requirements

### Pass/Fail option

#### When allowed
- Typically for electives
- Often not allowed for major requirements

#### How to apply
1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Select course grading option  
3) Submit pass/fail request before deadline  

---

## Credit limits

### Standard limits
- Full-time enrollment requires minimum credit load
- Maximum credits per semester set by school policy

---

## Step-by-step: How to request credit overload

1) Review school credit limit policy  
2) Contact academic advisor for approval  
3) Submit overload request form (if required)  
4) Receive confirmation before enrolling in extra credits  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Using pass/fail for required courses  
- Submitting overload request after deadline  
- Assuming approval is automatic  

---

## Consequences of violations
- Course removal
- Academic penalties
- Financial complications

---

## Where to manage grading and credit options
- Student Services Online (SSOL)
- Academic advising office

---

## School-specific notes
- Limits and policies vary by school.
