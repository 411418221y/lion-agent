## Summary
Explains move-in procedures, how to resolve room-related problems, and how students request room changes.

## Common student scenarios
- First day move-in  
- Room condition problems  
- Wanting to switch rooms or roommates  
- Temporary housing situations  

---

## Step-by-step: Move-in process

1) Check assigned move-in date and time in housing portal  
2) Arrive at designated residence hall  
3) Pick up keys or activate access credentials  
4) Complete room condition report  

---

## If there is a problem with your room

Common issues:
- Maintenance problems  
- Cleanliness concerns  
- Missing furniture  

**How to resolve**
1) Submit maintenance request through housing portal  
2) Contact residence hall office if urgent  

---

## Step-by-step: How to request a room change

1) Log into housing portal  
2) Select room change request option  
3) State reason for request  
4) Submit and wait for availability  

---

## Temporary housing

- May be provided during transitions  
- Usually short-term while assignments finalize  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Not documenting room issues early  
- Missing room change request windows  
- Informal roommate swaps without approval  

---

## Where to manage housing issues
- Columbia Housing portal  
- Residential services offices  

---

## School-specific notes
- Room change policies vary by residence.
