## Summary
Explains key billing deadlines, where students should make payments, and how to get help with billing-related questions.

## Common student scenarios
- Unsure when tuition or fees are due
- Missed a payment deadline
- Not sure who to contact for billing questions
- Seeing late fees or account holds

---

## Key billing deadlines

### Tuition and fee due dates
- Payments are typically due at the beginning of each semester.
- Specific deadlines are listed in Student Services Online (SSOL).

### Late payment consequences
- Late fees may be applied
- Account holds may block registration or transcript requests

---

## Step-by-step: How to check your billing balance and deadlines

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Navigate to the billing or account balance section  
3) Review:
   - Current charges
   - Payment due dates
   - Past due balances  

4) Download or view billing statements if needed

---

## Step-by-step: How to make a payment

1) Access the payment portal through SSOL  
2) Choose payment method:
   - Bank transfer
   - Credit/debit card (fees may apply)
   - Payment plan options  
3) Submit payment  
4) Save confirmation receipt

---

## Who to contact for billing help

### Student Financial Services (SFS)
- Questions about charges, payments, refunds, and holds
- Payment plan assistance

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Ignoring billing emails
- Missing deadlines during busy registration periods
- Assuming financial aid automatically covers all charges

---

## School-specific notes
- Professional schools may follow separate billing schedules.
