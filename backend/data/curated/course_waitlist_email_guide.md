## Summary
Provides a practical email structure students can use to request enrollment from instructors while on a course waitlist.

## Common student scenarios
- Course required for major or graduation
- No alternative sections available
- High position on waitlist but no movement

---

## Step-by-step: How to write a waitlist request email

### 1) Subject line
Example:
- Request for Enrollment Permission – [Course Number & Name]

### 2) Greeting
- Use formal greeting (Professor [Last Name])

### 3) Introduce yourself
Include:
- Full name
- Student ID
- Major or program
- Current academic year

### 4) State your request clearly
Example:
"I am currently on the waitlist for [Course Name] and would like to respectfully request permission to enroll."

### 5) Explain academic reason
Good reasons:
- Required for major or graduation timeline
- Prerequisite for upcoming courses
- Limited course offerings

### 6) Close politely
Thank them for time and consideration.

---

## Sample email template

Subject: Request for Enrollment Permission – ECON UN1011

Dear Professor [Last Name],

My name is [Your Name], and I am a [year] student majoring in [major]. I am currently on the waitlist for your course [Course Name and Number] and am writing to respectfully request permission to enroll.

This course is required for my academic plan, and I have limited alternative options this semester. I would greatly appreciate any consideration.

Thank you for your time and guidance.

Best regards,  
[Your Name]  
[UNI / Student ID]

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Sounding demanding
- Writing extremely long explanations
- Sending multiple follow-up emails quickly

---

## Where to submit permission (if approved)
- Through SSOL or registration system
- Using instructor-provided permission code or approval

---

## School-specific notes
- Some schools discourage instructor override requests.
