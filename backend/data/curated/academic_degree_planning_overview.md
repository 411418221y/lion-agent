## Summary
Explains how students track degree requirements and create a semester-by-semester academic plan toward graduation.

## Common student scenarios
- Unsure which requirements remain
- Worried about graduating on time
- Confused about major vs general requirements

---

## Core components of most Columbia degrees
- General education or core curriculum requirements  
- Major or concentration requirements  
- Elective credit totals  
- Residency and minimum credit rules  

---

## Step-by-step: How to plan your degree progress

### Step 1 – Review official program requirements
- Visit your major or program requirement page
- List all required courses

### Step 2 – Check your degree audit
- Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)
- Open degree audit or academic progress section

### Step 3 – Map remaining courses by semester
- Place prerequisites early
- Balance course difficulty

### Step 4 – Confirm plan with academic advisor
- Review graduation timeline
- Adjust if needed

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Delaying prerequisite chains  
- Overusing pass/fail credits  
- Assuming courses count without confirmation

## Tools that help planning
- Degree audit systems  
- Advising sessions

- Academic advising offices
