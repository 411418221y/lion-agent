## Summary
Explains how students apply for graduation and complete degree requirements before degree conferral.

## Graduation application process
1) Submit graduation application through Student Services Online (SSOL).
2) Confirm expected degree term.
3) Review degree audit for remaining requirements.
4) Resolve any outstanding academic issues.

## Degree clearance
- Academic departments review major requirements.
- Registrar verifies credit completion.
- Advisors may request additional documentation.

## Important deadlines
- Graduation application deadline per term
- Degree completion deadline
- Commencement participation cutoff

## Common issues
- Missing required credits
- Unapproved substitutions
- Late application submissions

## School-specific notes
- Deadlines may differ by school or program.
- Some schools require departmental sign-off.
