## Summary
Explains how students can dispute incorrect charges, request billing reviews, and track dispute outcomes.

## Common student scenarios
- Charged for a class that was dropped
- Incorrect housing or meal plan fees
- Duplicate payments or overcharges
- Financial aid not reflected on bill

---

## Step-by-step: How to dispute a billing charge

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Go to billing or account balance section  
3) Identify the incorrect charge  
4) Submit billing inquiry or dispute request  
5) Upload supporting documents if required  
6) Submit and save confirmation

---

## Supporting documents that may help
- Registration change confirmation
- Payment receipts
- Financial aid award letters
- Housing or meal plan confirmations

---

## What happens after submitting a dispute
- Billing office reviews request
- Student may be contacted for clarification
- Adjustments appear on account if approved

---

## Typical response timeline
- Initial review usually within several business days
- Complex cases may take longer

---

## If the charge is due soon

### Recommended action
- Submit dispute immediately
- Contact Student Financial Services to note pending review

(This may help avoid late penalties while under review)

---

## Where to get help
- Student Financial Services (billing support office)
- SSOL billing inquiry portal

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Waiting until after the deadline to dispute
- Not attaching proof
- Assuming charges will auto-correct

---

## School-specific notes
- Some schools require departmental confirmation for adjustments.
