## Summary
A universal step-by-step workflow to recommend a safe semester plan without hardcoding any single major.

## Step-by-step (universal)
Step 1) Identify the user’s major + year
- Example: "Economics major, first semester" / "CS major, second year"

Step 2) Give the official major link (must)
- Start here: https://bulletin.columbia.edu/general-studies/majors-concentrations/
- Then click their specific major page and provide that link

Step 3) Extract the 3 buckets from the major page
A) Core required courses
B) Foundations / introductory sequence (if listed)
C) Rules for electives/tracks (if listed)

Step 4) Choose 1 foundational “gateway” course first
- Rule: pick the earliest course in the sequence that unlocks later requirements
- If unclear, label as: "Likely gateway (confirm on major page)"

Step 5) Add 1 prerequisite-support course (if needed)
- Common types: math, stats, writing, language, lab co-req
- Only recommend if it helps satisfy prerequisites or degree requirements

Step 6) Add 1 degree/core requirement (if applicable)
- Use the user’s remaining requirements (if known) or suggest checking degree audit/advising

Step 7) Add 0–1 lighter elective (optional)
- Keep total workload realistic

Step 8) Verify prerequisites before finalizing
- Use course listing + prereq checks (see separate prereq doc)
- Don’t recommend a course if prereqs are not met

Step 9) Registration actions
- Add/Drop timing, waitlists, permissions
- Registration guide: https://registrar.columbia.edu/content/class-registration-students

## Output format suggestion (for your product UI)
- Recommended Courses (3–5)
- Why each course (1 sentence)
- Prereq check checklist
- Official links (major page + registration guide)
