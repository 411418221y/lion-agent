## Summary
Explains why students are placed on course waitlists and what actions can improve their chances of enrolling.

## Common student scenarios
- Added to waitlist instead of enrolled
- Unsure if waitlist will move
- Trying to secure required courses for major

---

## Common reasons students are waitlisted

### Course capacity reached
- All seats filled

### Priority enrollment groups
- Majors, seniors, or specific programs receive priority

### Required approvals missing
- Instructor or department permission required

### Scheduling conflicts
- Overlapping course times block enrollment

---

## Step-by-step: How to improve waitlist chances

1) Remain enrolled on the waitlist  
2) Monitor seat openings regularly  
3) Attend first few class sessions if allowed  
4) Contact instructor politely explaining academic need  
5) Verify no scheduling conflicts exist  

---

## When to contact the instructor

Best situations:
- Course required for major or graduation timeline
- Limited alternative options

What to include:
- Student ID
- Course number
- Academic reason for request

---

## What usually does NOT help
- Repeated emails
- Dropping and re-adding waitlist repeatedly
- Waiting passively without checking system updates

---

## Where to track waitlist status
- Student Services Online (SSOL)
- Course registration system

---

## School-specific notes
- Some schools manually manage waitlists.
