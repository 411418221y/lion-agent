## Summary
Explains how students receive, pick up, and manage mail and packages on campus.

## Package delivery process
1) Packages are delivered to designated campus mail centers.
2) Students receive email notification when items arrive.
3) Bring student ID to pick up packages.
4) Sign for received items if required.

## Mailbox services
- Some residences have assigned mailboxes
- Letters and small packages delivered directly

## Common issues and solutions

### Missing package
- Check delivery confirmation
- Visit campus mail center
- Contact mail services staff

### Delayed pickup
- Packages held for limited time
- Unclaimed items may be returned

## Restrictions
- Oversized packages may have special pickup locations
- Prohibited items may be rejected

## School-specific notes
- Pickup locations vary by residence hall.
