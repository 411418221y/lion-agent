## Summary
Explains transportation options available to Columbia students including campus shuttles and public transit access.

## Campus shuttle services
- Free shuttles connecting main campus and nearby facilities
- Operate on scheduled routes and hours
- Real-time tracking available online

## Public transportation
- Subway and bus access near campus
- Student discounts may apply through transit programs

## Bike access
- Campus bike racks and storage areas
- City bike-sharing services nearby

## Common issues and solutions

### Missed shuttle
- Check real-time shuttle tracker
- Use alternate campus routes or public transit

### Service changes
- Monitor transportation service announcements

## Where to find schedules
- Columbia transportation website
- Shuttle tracking apps or portals

## School-specific notes
- Some campuses have separate shuttle systems.
