## Summary
Lists common documents students must submit for housing applications, assignments, and move-in approval.

## Common student scenarios
- Housing application marked incomplete  
- Move-in delayed due to missing paperwork  
- Confusion about required forms  

---

## Commonly required documents

### For housing applications
- Student identification information  
- Emergency contact details  
- Housing agreement forms  

### For move-in approval
- Immunization or health clearance forms (if required)  
- Proof of enrollment  

### For special housing requests
- Medical accommodation forms  
- Family or partner housing documentation  

---

## Step-by-step: How to submit housing documents

1) Log into housing portal  
2) Open application or document upload section  
3) Upload required forms  
4) Confirm submission status  

---

## Tips for smooth processing

- Upload clear scans or PDFs  
- Label documents properly  
- Submit before deadlines  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Submitting partial forms  
- Missing signatures  
- Waiting until move-in week  

---

## What happens after submission
- Housing office reviews materials  
- Students contacted if corrections needed  
- Approval reflected in housing portal  

---

## Where to manage housing paperwork
- Columbia Housing portal  
- Housing services website  

---

## School-specific notes
- Some programs require additional housing forms.
