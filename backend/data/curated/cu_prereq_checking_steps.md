## Summary
Explains how students check course prerequisites and request approval when requirements are not automatically satisfied.

## Common student scenarios
- Blocked from enrolling due to missing prerequisite
- Completed equivalent course elsewhere
- Transfer credit not reflected

---

## Step-by-step: How to check prerequisites

1) Open Course Directory or registration system  
2) Select desired course  
3) Review listed prerequisite requirements  
4) Compare with completed coursework  

---

## If prerequisites are already completed but system blocks enrollment

### What to do
1) Gather proof of completed coursework  
   - Transcript  
   - Course syllabus (if from another institution)  
2) Contact department or instructor  
3) Request prerequisite override approval  

---

## If you have not completed the prerequisite

Options may include:
- Enroll in prerequisite course first  
- Request instructor permission (rarely granted)  
- Take equivalent course if approved  

---

## Where approvals are usually processed
- Department administrative office  
- Instructor permission systems  
- SSOL override forms  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Ignoring prerequisite listings  
- Assuming transfer credits auto-apply  
- Waiting until registration deadline  

---

## School-specific notes
- Some schools enforce prerequisites strictly.
