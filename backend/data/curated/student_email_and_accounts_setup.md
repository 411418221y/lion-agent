## Summary
Explains how students activate and manage Columbia University email and online system accounts.

## University email

### Purpose
- Official communication from instructors and university offices
- Financial aid, billing, registration, and emergency notices

### How to activate
1) Receive UNI (University Network ID)
2) Log into Columbia account activation portal
3) Set password and security options
4) Access email through Columbia mail system

## Student systems linked to email
- Student Services Online (SSOL)
- Course registration platforms
- Financial services portals
- Library systems

## Common issues and solutions

### Cannot log in
- Reset password through IT portal
- Verify UNI activation

### Not receiving important emails
- Check spam folder
- Confirm correct forwarding settings

## Where to get help
- Columbia University IT Help Desk
- Online account support portal

## School-specific notes
- Some professional programs use additional platforms.
