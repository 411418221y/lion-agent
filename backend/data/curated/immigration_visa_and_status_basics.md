## Summary
Explains basic visa responsibilities, common requests, and how international students maintain legal status at Columbia University.

## Common visa types
- F-1 student visa
- J-1 exchange visitor visa

## Core responsibilities
- Maintain full-time enrollment
- Keep passport valid
- Report address changes
- Follow employment authorization rules

## Common student requests

### I-20 or DS-2019 updates
**How to request**
1) Log into ISSO student portal
2) Submit update request form
3) Upload required documents
4) Receive updated document electronically or by pickup

### Travel signature
**How to request**
1) Submit travel signature request online
2) Wait for processing confirmation
3) Download or collect signed document

### Employment authorization (CPT/OPT)
**Steps**
1) Confirm eligibility
2) Attend required workshop (if applicable)
3) Submit application through ISSO portal
4) Receive authorization before working

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Dropping below full-time status without approval
- Working without authorization
- Missing document expiration dates

## Where to get help
- ISSO advising appointments
- ISSO online request system

## School-specific notes
- Some programs have additional compliance steps.
