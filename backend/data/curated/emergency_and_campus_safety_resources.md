## Summary
Provides guidance on emergency services, campus safety resources, and how students can get immediate help.

## Emergency situations
- Medical emergencies
- Threats or unsafe incidents
- Fire or building hazards

**What to do**
1) Call emergency services (911) immediately
2) Contact Columbia Public Safety
3) Follow campus emergency alerts

## Campus safety services
- 24/7 Public Safety patrols
- Campus escort services
- Emergency call boxes

## Reporting incidents
- Online incident reporting forms
- In-person Public Safety offices

## Emergency notifications
- University-wide alert system via email and text
- Safety announcements

## Support resources
- Counseling services
- Victim advocacy services

## School-specific notes
- Some campuses have localized safety offices.
