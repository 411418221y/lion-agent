## Summary
Explains how Columbia dining plans work, how to use meal swipes, and how to manage meal plan changes.

## Meal plan basics
- Prepaid meal swipes for campus dining locations
- Dining dollars or flex balance for additional purchases
- Plans vary by residence and student status

## How to use meal swipes
1) Present student ID at dining location
2) Swipe for meal entry or purchase
3) Balance updates automatically

## Changing meal plans
- Allowed during designated change periods
- Requests submitted through dining services portal
- Upgrades usually allowed anytime

## Common issues and solutions

### Ran out of swipes
- Use dining dollars or personal payment
- Upgrade plan if within change window

### Incorrect charges
- Contact dining services support

## School-specific notes
- Some residences require meal plan enrollment.
