## Summary
Explains which Columbia University fees cannot be waived or removed and helps students avoid unnecessary dispute attempts.

## Common student scenarios
- Trying to waive mandatory university fees
- Requesting removal of standard service charges
- Confusion about automatic charges

---

## Fees that are typically non-waivable

### Mandatory university fees
- Student services fees  
- Health and campus facility fees  
- Technology or administrative fees  

### Enrollment-based charges
- Tuition tied to registered credits  
- Program-required fees  

### Insurance charges (if waiver deadline missed)
- Automatically billed once deadline passes

---

## Step-by-step: How to confirm if a fee is non-waivable

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Open billing statement details  
3) Click charge description  
4) Compare with Student Financial Services fee policy page  

---

## When fee removal may still be possible

Some charges may be reviewed if:
- Billing system error occurred
- Enrollment status was recorded incorrectly
- Approved waiver submitted on time but not processed

**Recommended action**
- Submit billing inquiry with documentation

---

## Fees students often mistakenly try to dispute
- Mandatory service fees  
- Late fees caused by missed deadlines  
- Required program charges  

(These are rarely reversed)

---

## How to avoid unnecessary charges

- Monitor billing emails regularly  
- Submit waivers before deadlines  
- Review account balance each semester  

---

## Where to get clarification
- Student Financial Services (SFS)  
- Billing inquiry system in SSOL
