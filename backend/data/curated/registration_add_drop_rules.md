## Summary
Explains how students can add or drop courses after initial registration and the academic and financial impact of schedule changes.

## Add period rules
- Students may add courses during the official add/drop window.
- Some courses require instructor or department approval.
- Closed courses may require waitlist or special permission.

## Drop period rules
- Courses dropped during add/drop do not appear on transcripts.
- Dropping after the deadline may result in a "W" (withdrawal).

## Financial impact
- Tuition adjustments may apply based on drop timing.
- Late drops may not qualify for refunds.

## Important deadlines
- Add/drop deadline (varies each semester)
- Withdrawal deadline

## School-specific notes
- GS students may have extended add/drop periods in some terms.
- Professional schools may use separate policies.
