## Summary
Explains which students may qualify for fee waivers and how to apply for billing-related waivers at Columbia University.

## Common student scenarios
- Trying to waive mandatory fees
- Unsure if personal insurance qualifies for waiver
- Missing waiver deadlines
- Waiver request denied

---

## Fees commonly eligible for waiver
- Health insurance plan charges (with approved alternative coverage)
- Certain program-based fees (when exemptions apply)

## Fees usually NOT eligible
- Tuition
- Standard university service fees
- Late payment penalties

---

## Step-by-step: How to apply for a waiver

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Navigate to waiver or fee adjustment section  
3) Select fee type for waiver  
4) Upload required supporting documents  
5) Submit application  
6) Monitor approval status

---

## Typical waiver requirements
- Proof of alternative insurance coverage
- Eligibility verification forms
- Deadline compliance

---

## What happens after submission
- Waiver reviewed by billing office
- Student notified of approval or denial
- Approved waivers removed from account balance

---

## Consequences of missing deadlines
- Automatic billing
- No retroactive waiver in most cases

---

## Tips for approval success
- Submit early
- Upload complete documents
- Double-check eligibility rules

---

## Where to get help
- Student Financial Services (SFS)
- Waiver submission portal in SSOL

---

## School-specific notes
- Waiver policies may differ by program.
