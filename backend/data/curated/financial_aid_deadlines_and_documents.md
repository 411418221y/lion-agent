## Summary
Lists common financial aid deadlines and the documents students must submit to receive aid.

## Common deadlines
- FAFSA and CSS Profile submission deadlines
- Financial aid renewal deadlines each academic year
- Verification document submission deadlines

## Frequently required documents
- Tax returns and W-2 forms
- Proof of income
- Household size verification
- Citizenship or residency documents

## Verification process
- Some students are selected for aid verification
- Additional documentation may be requested

## Consequences of missing deadlines
- Delayed aid disbursement
- Reduced eligibility
- Loss of certain grants

## School-specific notes
- Deadlines vary by school and student type.
- Graduate and professional programs may follow separate schedules.
