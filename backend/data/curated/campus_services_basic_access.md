## Summary
Explains how students access core campus services and how to resolve common access problems.

## Student ID cards

### Normal use
- Required for building entry, libraries, dining, and services.

### Lost or damaged card – what to do
1) Log into Columbia ID services portal.
2) Report card as lost or stolen.
3) Request replacement card.
4) Pay replacement fee if required.
5) Pick up new card at designated campus office.

### Common issues
- Access temporarily disabled after reporting loss.
- Fees may apply for replacement.

## Library access
- Use active student ID to enter libraries.
- Digital access through Columbia library portal.

## Gym and recreation facilities
- Student ID grants entry to most facilities.
- Some locations require separate reservations.

## Where to get help
- Campus Services Office
- Online ID management portal

## School-specific notes
- Some programs issue additional access credentials.
