## Summary
Explains Columbia University’s student health insurance requirements and the process for waiving coverage.

## Insurance requirement
- Most students are automatically enrolled in Columbia’s health insurance plan.
- Coverage applies each academic year unless waived.

## Waiver eligibility
- Students with comparable external insurance may apply for a waiver.
- Insurance must meet Columbia’s coverage standards.

## Waiver process
1) Submit waiver application online.
2) Upload proof of insurance.
3) Receive approval or denial notification.

## Deadlines
- Waiver deadlines each semester or academic year
- Late submissions may not be accepted

## Consequences of missing waiver
- Automatic enrollment and billing
- No retroactive removal

## School-specific notes
- Coverage rules may differ by program.
