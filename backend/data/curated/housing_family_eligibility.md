## Summary
Explains who qualifies for family or partner housing and how to apply for these specialized housing options.

## Common student scenarios
- Living with spouse or partner  
- Housing with dependents  
- Unsure if relationship qualifies  

---

## Who is typically eligible

### Family housing
- Married students  
- Students with legal dependents  

### Partner housing
- Domestic partners (documentation required)

---

## Common eligibility requirements
- Proof of relationship  
- Enrollment verification  
- Additional housing agreements  

---

## Step-by-step: How to apply for family or partner housing

1) Log into housing portal  
2) Select family/partner housing option  
3) Upload required proof documents  
4) Submit housing application  
5) Monitor assignment status  

---

## Documents often required
- Marriage certificate  
- Domestic partnership affidavit  
- Dependent documentation  

---

## Important considerations
- Limited availability  
- Separate waitlists may apply  
- Early application strongly recommended  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Applying late  
- Missing relationship proof  
- Assuming eligibility without confirmation  

---

## Where to get eligibility confirmation
- Housing office  
- Housing portal documentation section  

---

## School-specific notes
- Eligibility rules vary by housing type.
