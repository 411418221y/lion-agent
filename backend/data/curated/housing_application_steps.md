## Summary
Explains how students apply for campus housing, track application status, and respond to housing offers.

## Common student scenarios
- First-time housing application
- Unsure when housing opens
- Missed housing deadline
- Confused about room assignment process

---

## Step-by-step: How to apply for housing

1) Log into housing portal through Columbia student systems  
2) Select housing application for upcoming term  
3) Complete:
   - Personal information  
   - Room preferences  
   - Lifestyle questions (if applicable)  

4) Submit application before deadline  
5) Save confirmation

---

## After submitting application

Students typically:
- Wait for housing assignment notification  
- Receive offer via email or portal  
- Accept or decline by stated deadline  

---

## Important deadlines

- Housing application opening date  
- Housing application deadline  
- Offer acceptance deadline  

(Missing deadlines often removes housing eligibility)

---

## If you missed the deadline

Possible options:
- Join housing waitlist  
- Seek off-campus housing  
- Contact housing office for special circumstances  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Waiting until last day to apply  
- Ignoring housing emails  
- Forgetting to accept assigned housing  

---

## Where to apply and track status
- Columbia Housing portal  
- University housing services website  

---

## School-specific notes
- Some graduate programs use separate housing processes.
