## Summary
Lists the information students typically need when requesting enrollment approval or submitting waitlist-related forms.

## Common student scenarios
- Permission request rejected due to missing details
- Delays in waitlist processing
- Confusion about required forms

---

## Information commonly required

### Student details
- Full name  
- Student ID or UNI  
- School and major  

### Course details
- Course number and title  
- Section number  
- Instructor name  

### Academic justification
- Requirement for major or graduation  
- Prerequisite sequencing  
- Limited alternatives  

---

## Step-by-step: How to prepare a complete waitlist request

1) Gather all required student information  
2) Confirm correct course and section numbers  
3) Clearly state academic reason  
4) Attach any required forms if applicable  
5) Submit through designated system or email  

---

## Optional supporting materials
- Degree audit showing requirement
- Advisor confirmation email

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Forgetting section number  
- Vague academic reasons  
- Missing student identification  

---

## Where requests are usually processed
- Instructor approval systems  
- Department enrollment forms  
- Student Services Online (SSOL)

## School-specific notes
- Some departments require formal override forms.
