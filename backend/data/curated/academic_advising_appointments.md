## Summary
Explains how students book academic advising appointments and prepare to get the most value from each meeting.

## Common student scenarios
- Unsure which courses to take next semester
- Concerned about degree progress
- Needing approval for overloads or special requests

---

## Step-by-step: How to schedule an advising appointment

1) Log into your school’s advising scheduling system or portal  
2) Select advisor and appointment type  
3) Choose available time slot  
4) Confirm appointment and save details  

---

## How to prepare for advising sessions

Bring:
- Degree audit or progress report  
- Course plan for upcoming semesters  
- List of questions or concerns  

---

## Topics advisors typically help with
- Course sequencing  
- Graduation planning  
- Academic challenges  
- Policy clarification  

---

## If no appointments are available
- Check scheduling system regularly  
- Email advising office for urgent issues  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Attending unprepared  
- Waiting until deadlines approach  
- Assuming advisors monitor progress automatically  

---

## Where advising happens
- Online scheduling systems  
- School advising offices  

---

## School-specific notes
- Advising structures differ by school.
