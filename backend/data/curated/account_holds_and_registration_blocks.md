## Summary
Explains common account holds that prevent registration or access to services and how students can remove them.

## Common types of holds

### Financial holds
- Unpaid tuition or fees
- Past due balances

**How to resolve**
1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)
2) Review outstanding balance
3) Submit payment or payment plan
4) Confirm hold removal

---

### Academic holds
- Missing advising approval
- Unsatisfied academic requirements

**How to resolve**
1) Contact academic advisor
2) Complete required approvals
3) Verify hold cleared in SSOL

---

### Administrative holds
- Missing documents
- Immunization or compliance records

**How to resolve**
1) Upload required documents
2) Contact appropriate campus office
3) Confirm clearance

## Where to check holds
- Student Services Online (SSOL)

## Consequences of unresolved holds
- Inability to register for courses
- Blocked transcript requests
- Delayed graduation processing

## School-specific notes
- Some schools impose additional approval holds.
