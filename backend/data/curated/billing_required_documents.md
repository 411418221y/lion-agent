## Summary
Lists common documents students must submit for billing requests, waivers, and dispute reviews.

## Common student scenarios
- Billing dispute requests delayed
- Waiver applications rejected
- Payments not properly credited

(Most issues happen because required documents were missing or incorrect.)

---

## Frequently required documents

### For billing disputes
- Proof of payment receipts  
- Registration change confirmations  
- Financial aid award letters  
- Housing or meal plan confirmations  

### For waiver requests
- Insurance proof (for health fee waivers)  
- Eligibility verification forms  

### For special billing reviews
- Enrollment status documents  
- Program approval forms  

---

## Step-by-step: How to submit billing documents

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Open billing inquiry or waiver section  
3) Upload required documents  
4) Confirm successful submission  

---

## Tips for faster processing

- Upload clear PDF or image files  
- Label documents properly  
- Submit before stated deadlines  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid

- Submitting screenshots instead of official documents  
- Missing required pages of forms  
- Uploading after deadlines  

---

## What happens after submission

- Billing office reviews materials  
- Student may receive follow-up requests  
- Account updated once approved  

---

## Where to get help
- Student Financial Services support office  
- SSOL document upload portal  

---

## School-specific notes
- Some programs require additional verification forms.
