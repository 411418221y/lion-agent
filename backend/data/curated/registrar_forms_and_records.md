## Summary
Explains how students request official documents, submit registrar forms, and update academic records.

## Common student scenarios
- Need official transcript
- Require enrollment verification
- Updating personal information
- Submitting academic forms

---

## Common registrar requests

### Official transcripts
- For jobs, graduate school, scholarships

### Enrollment verification
- Proof of student status

### Personal information updates
- Name changes
- Address updates

### Academic forms
- Leave of absence
- Program changes
- Graduation forms

---

## Step-by-step: How to request transcripts

1) Log into Student Services Online (SSOL)  
2) Navigate to transcript request section  
3) Select delivery method (electronic or mail)  
4) Submit request  

---

## Step-by-step: How to obtain enrollment verification

1) Access SSOL  
2) Download enrollment verification letter  
3) Submit where required  

---

## Step-by-step: How to submit registrar forms

1) Locate required form on registrar website  
2) Complete form details  
3) Submit electronically or via designated office  

---

## Common mistakes to avoid
- Waiting until deadlines  
- Using unofficial transcripts  
- Submitting incomplete forms  

---

## Where to manage records
- Student Services Online (SSOL)  
- Registrar office websites

## School-specific notes
- Processing times vary by school.
