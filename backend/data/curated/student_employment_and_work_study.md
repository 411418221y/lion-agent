## Summary
Explains student employment opportunities including on-campus jobs and federal work-study programs.

## On-campus employment
- Part-time jobs within university departments
- Open to most enrolled students
- Paid hourly

## Federal work-study
- Need-based financial aid program
- Limited to eligible students
- Earnings do not reduce aid eligibility

## How to find jobs
- Columbia student employment portals
- Department postings
- Career Education Center resources

## Hiring process
- Resume submission
- Interview
- Employment authorization forms

## School-specific notes
- Some schools restrict work hours per week.
- International students may have additional authorization requirements.
